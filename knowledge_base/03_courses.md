# Courses offered at Keshav Mahavidyalaya

Keshav Mahavidyalaya offers undergraduate honours and programme courses, plus certificate and
diploma courses in foreign languages.

## Undergraduate programmes
| Programme | Duration | Approx. Total Seats |
|---|---|---|
| B.Com (Hons.) | 4 Years (NEP) | 192 |
| B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science | 4 Years (NEP) | 115 |
| B.Sc. (Hons.) Electronics | 4 Years (NEP) | 40 |
| B.Sc. (Hons.) Mathematics | 4 Years (NEP) | 59 |
| B.Sc. (Hons.) Physics | 4 Years (NEP) | -- |
| B.Sc. (Prog.) Mathematical Science | 4 Years (NEP) | 59 |
| B.Sc. (Prog.) Physical Science with Computer Science | 4 Years (NEP) | 59 |
| B.A. (Hons.) Psychology | 4 Years (NEP) | 40 |
| Bachelor of Management Studies (BMS) | 3 Years (6 semesters) | 59 |

Notes:
- Under the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, most UG programmes follow a 4-year
  undergraduate format with multiple exit options.
- BMS (Bachelor of Management Studies) is a professional programme. It was introduced by the
  University of Delhi in 1987 and has been taught at Keshav Mahavidyalaya since 2007.
- Seats are distributed across categories: UR, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, plus supernumerary
  categories like PwBD (PH), CW, KM and FS/sports.
- Meritorious students of B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science can get direct admission to
  M.Sc. Computer Science at the University of Delhi.

## Certificate courses in foreign languages
| Course | Duration | Seats | Eligibility |

| Certificate Course in German Language | 1 Year | 50 | 10+2 / Graduation / Post-Graduation with minimum 45% aggregate |
| Certificate Course in French Language | 1 Year | 50 | 10+2 / Graduation / Post-Graduation with minimum 45% aggregate |

## Diploma courses in foreign languages
| Course | Duration | Seats | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diploma Course in German Language | 1 Year | 25 | Must have passed the Certificate Examination of Delhi University in the language concerned (or equivalent) |
| Diploma Course in French Language | 1 Year | 25 | Must have passed the Certificate Examination of Delhi University in the language concerned (or equivalent) |

For diploma admission, candidates who obtained their certificate from another institution one
year before the year of admission may need to appear in an admission test conducted by the college.

## Departments
The college has departments in Commerce, Computer Science, Economics, Electronics, English,
Hindi, Mathematics, Physics, Psychology, and foreign languages (French and German).
