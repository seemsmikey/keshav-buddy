# Facilities, scholarships and student life at Keshav Mahavidyalaya

## Infrastructure and facilities
- Automated library with more than 32,000 books and reading room facilities.
- Well-equipped science and computer laboratories.
- Wi-Fi connectivity across the campus.
- Seminar hall with LCD projector.
- E-enabled, air-conditioned lecture halls.
- Sports facilities including a cricket ground and basketball court.
- Canteen / cafeteria.
- Common rooms for students.
- Medical / first-aid facilities.

## Hostel
- The college provides hostel facilities for female students.
- Hostel facilities for male students are not available directly at the college.
- For hostel enquiries, contact the college office.

## Scholarships
- The college helps students avail various scholarships such as:
  - Delhi University / UGC scholarships
  - SC/ST and OBC scholarships (Government of India / NCT Delhi)
  - EWS scholarships
  - SSV Excellence & Empowerment Scholarship
  - Merit-based and merit-cum-means scholarships
- Students should monitor the college notices and the scholarship portals for deadlines.

## Student support
- Anti-Ragging Cell: anti-ragging@keshav.du.ac.in
- Grievance redressal and student welfare committees
- Equal Opportunity Cell for students with disabilities
- NCC, NSS and various cultural and departmental societies

## Co-curricular and committees
- Various departmental societies organise academic and cultural activities.
- The college publishes an annual magazine, "Keshav Horizon".
- Students participate in sports, cultural festivals and inter-college competitions.

## Library
- The library is automated and supports lending, reference and reading room services.
- More than 32,000 books are available.
- Contact: library@keshav.du.ac.in
