# Placements at Keshav Mahavidyalaya

The college has an active Placement Cell that works to connect students with recruiters and
internship opportunities.

## Placement highlights (2024-25)
- Number of companies that offered jobs: around 20
- Number of companies that offered internships: around 13
- Highest salary package: approximately Rs. 8,50,000 per annum
- Average salary package: approximately Rs. 6,08,035 per annum
- Total domestic placements: around 11

Note: Placement statistics vary every year and depend on the batch, programme and market
conditions.

## Placement Cell
- The Placement Cell organises internship drives, job fairs, career counselling sessions,
  resume-building workshops and mock interviews.
- Students from BMS, B.Com (Hons.), B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science and other programmes are
  eligible to participate in placement activities.
- For placement queries, contact the Placement Cell at placementcell@keshav.du.ac.in.
