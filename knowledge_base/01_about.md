# About Keshav Mahavidyalaya

Keshav Mahavidyalaya (KMV) is a constituent college of the University of Delhi.
It was established in the year 1994 and is fully funded by the Government of NCT of Delhi.
It is one of the youngest constituent colleges of the University of Delhi.

## Key facts
- Full name: Keshav Mahavidyalaya (University of Delhi)
- Established: 1994
- Funding: Fully funded by the Government of NCT of Delhi
- Affiliation: University of Delhi
- Accreditation: NAAC Accredited 'A' Grade (Cycle 2)
- NIRF Ranking: Ranked #95 among colleges in India (NIRF 2025)
- Location: H-4-5 Zone, Pitampura, Near Sainik Vihar, Delhi - 110034
- Type: Public / Government co-educational college
- It is an undergraduate college and does not offer postgraduate (PG) or PhD programmes.

## About the campus
The college campus is located in Pitampura, North West Delhi. The college offers a modern
infrastructure including well-equipped laboratories, Wi-Fi connectivity, a seminar hall with
LCD projector, e-enabled lecture halls, an automated library and sports facilities such as a
cricket ground and basketball court.

The college is committed to academic excellence, holistic development and empowering young
minds through quality higher education.
