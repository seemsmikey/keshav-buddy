# Admissions at Keshav Mahavidyalaya

## Undergraduate (UG) admission process
Admission to all undergraduate programmes at Keshav Mahavidyalaya is done through the
centralised Delhi University Common Seat Allocation System (DU CSAS).

The steps of the UG admission process are:
1. Apply for and appear in the CUET UG (Common University Entrance Test - Undergraduate)
   conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA).
2. Register on the Delhi University CSAS portal (admission.uod.ac.in) using your CUET UG scores.
3. Fill in your programme and college preferences, including Keshav Mahavidyalaya.
4. Pay the application fee and submit your application.
5. The University of Delhi releases cut-offs / allocation rounds. If you are allocated a seat at
   Keshav Mahavidyalaya, you must accept it on the CSAS portal.
6. Pay the admission fee and get your documents verified to confirm your admission.

## Eligibility
- Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Class XII) from a recognised board.
- Candidates must have a valid CUET UG score for the relevant programme.
- Programme-specific subject requirements apply. For example, BMS requires Mathematics /
  Business Mathematics / an equivalent paper.

## Documents required
- Class X and Class XII marksheets and certificates
- CUET UG scorecard
- Passport-size photographs
- Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwBD), if applicable
- Transfer / Migration certificate
- Character certificate
- Government photo ID proof

## Important note
Admission dates, cut-offs and the number of rounds change every year. For the latest and
official admission schedule, always refer to the Delhi University CSAS portal and the college
website at https://keshav.du.ac.in. You can also email the admissions office at
admissions@keshav.du.ac.in.

## Certificate and Diploma course admission
Admission to the Certificate and Diploma courses in French and German is handled directly by
the college (not through CUET/CSAS). See the Courses section for eligibility details.
