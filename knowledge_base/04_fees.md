# Fee structure at Keshav Mahavidyalaya

Fees are revised yearly. The figures below are from the official Consolidated Programme Fee
Structure for 2026-2027 (amounts are in Indian Rupees) and exclude the University examination fee.

## Undergraduate programme fees (annual, General / Others category)
| Programme | GEN & Others | SC / ST | PwBD |
|---|---|---|---|
| B.Com (Hons.) | 14,630 | 14,450 | 350 |
| B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science | 29,450 | 29,270 | 350 |
| B.Sc. (Hons.) Electronics | 14,630 | 14,450 | 350 |
| B.Sc. (Hons.) Mathematics | 14,630 | 14,450 | 350 |
| B.Sc. (Prog.) Mathematical Science | 14,630 | 14,450 | 350 |
| Bachelor of Management Studies (BMS) | 20,030 | 19,850 | 350 |
| B.Sc. (Hons.) Physics | 14,630 | 14,450 | 350 |
| B.Sc. (Prog.) Physical Science with Computer Science | 14,630 | 14,450 | 350 |
| B.A. (Hons.) Psychology | 14,630 | 14,450 | 350 |

- OBC, EWS, CW, KM and Sports/ECA categories are generally charged the same as the General
  category, after applicable classification.
- PwBD students pay a concessional fee (approx. Rs. 350).
- Orphan category students pay a nominal fee (approx. Rs. 10).

## Certificate and Diploma course fees
| Course | Fee |
|---|---|
| Certificate Course in French / German (College Students) | Rs. 7,200 per year |
| Certificate Course in French / German (Outside Students) | Rs. 9,200 per year |
| Diploma Course in French / German | Rs. 12,000 per year |

## Fee payment
- Fees are paid online through the college's online payment portal.
- The college also has an online payment option for the Association fee.

For the latest and exact fee structure, please refer to the college website at
https://keshav.du.ac.in under Admissions > Fee Structure, or email admissions@keshav.du.ac.in.
