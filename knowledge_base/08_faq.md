# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Keshav Mahavidyalaya Helpdesk

Q: What is Keshav Mahavidyalaya?
A: Keshav Mahavidyalaya (KMV) is a constituent college of the University of Delhi, established
in 1994 and fully funded by the Government of NCT of Delhi. It is a NAAC 'A' Grade accredited
undergraduate college located in Pitampura, Delhi.

Q: Where is Keshav Mahavidyalaya located?
A: The college is at H-4-5 Zone, Pitampura, Near Sainik Vihar, Delhi - 110034.

Q: How can I contact the college?
A: Call 011-27018805, or email principal@keshav.du.ac.in. For admissions, email
admissions@keshav.du.ac.in. The website is https://keshav.du.ac.in.

Q: What courses are offered at Keshav Mahavidyalaya?
A: UG courses include B.Com (Hons.), B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science, B.Sc. (Hons.) Electronics,
B.Sc. (Hons.) Mathematics, B.Sc. (Hons.) Physics, B.Sc. (Prog.) Mathematical Science, B.Sc.
(Prog.) Physical Science with Computer Science, B.A. (Hons.) Psychology and BMS. The college
also offers Certificate and Diploma courses in French and German.

Q: How do I get admission to Keshav Mahavidyalaya?
A: Admission to UG courses is through CUET UG and the Delhi University Common Seat Allocation
System (DU CSAS). You must appear in CUET UG, register on the DU CSAS portal, and fill
Keshav Mahavidyalaya in your preferences.

Q: Is CUET required for admission?
A: Yes. A valid CUET UG score is required for admission to undergraduate programmes.

Q: What is the fee for B.Com (Hons.)?
A: The annual fee for B.Com (Hons.) for 2026-2027 is approximately Rs. 14,630 for the General
category (excluding examination fees).

Q: What is the fee for B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science?
A: The annual fee for B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science for 2026-2027 is approximately Rs. 29,450
for the General category (excluding examination fees).

Q: Does the college provide hostel facilities?
A: Yes, hostel facilities are available for female students. Hostel facilities for male
students are not available at the college.

Q: Does Keshav Mahavidyalaya offer postgraduate courses?
A: No. Keshav Mahavidyalaya is an undergraduate college and does not offer PG or PhD programmes.

Q: Does the college have a placement cell?
A: Yes. The college has an active Placement Cell that organises placements and internships.
In 2024-25, around 20 companies offered jobs with a highest package of about Rs. 8.5 lakh per annum.

Q: How many seats are available in B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science?
A: There are 115 seats in B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science, distributed across categories.

Q: Does the college offer scholarships?
A: Yes. Students can avail scholarships such as SC/ST and OBC scholarships, EWS scholarships,
merit scholarships, the SSV Excellence & Empowerment Scholarship and Delhi University schemes.

Q: Is there a French or German language course?
A: Yes. The college offers Certificate (1 year) and Diploma (1 year) courses in French and
German, with separate eligibility and fees.

Q: What is the annual fee for the Certificate course in French/German?
A: Rs. 7,200 per year for college students and Rs. 9,200 per year for outside students.

Q: What library facilities are available?
A: The college has an automated library with more than 32,000 books and reading room facilities.
Contact library@keshav.du.ac.in for library queries.

Q: How do I report a ragging incident?
A: You can email the Anti-Ragging Cell at antiragging@keshav.du.ac.in.

Q: What is the NIRF ranking of Keshav Mahavidyalaya?
A: Keshav Mahavidyalaya was ranked #95 among colleges in India in NIRF 2025.

Q: Is Keshav Mahavidyalaya a government college?
A: Yes. It is a government-funded constituent college of the University of Delhi, fully funded
by the Government of NCT of Delhi.
