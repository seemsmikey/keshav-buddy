## How to reach the campus
The college is located in Pitampura, North West Delhi, near Sainik Vihar.
The nearest Delhi Metro stations are on the Red Line (e.g., Pitampura / Kohat Enclave area),
from where the campus is a short auto-rickshaw or cab ride away. Local buses also serve the
Pitampura area.

## Office hours
The college administrative office generally functions Monday to Saturday during regular
working hours (approximately 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM), excluding gazetted holidays. It is best to
call ahead at 011-27018805 before visiting for admissions or administrative work.
